Staten Island is mostly houses: detached homes, attached two-families and some apartment buildings near the ferry. That means most of our work here is bigger than one room. Families often repaint a whole floor or the whole house at once, and sellers want a clean, neutral look before listing.

## Whole-house interiors

When we paint a full house, we plan the order of rooms so you can keep living there. Ceilings go first, then walls, then trim and doors. Stairways and tall entries get the ladders and floor protection they need.

## Preparing a house for sale

Buyers notice scuffs, nail holes and tired colors. We patch and sand the wear, then paint in colors that photograph well. Tell us your listing date and we will work to it.

## Older homes near St. George

The older houses near the ferry often have plaster walls and original woodwork. These need more prep, and we include it in your price instead of billing extra by the hour.

Every job comes with one written, flat-rate price that covers prep, paint, labor and cleanup.
