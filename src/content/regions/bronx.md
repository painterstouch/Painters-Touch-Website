The Bronx has a wide range of homes, from Art Deco apartment buildings along the Grand Concourse to co-ops and hillside houses in Riverdale and one- and two-family homes in Throgs Neck. Each needs a different approach.

## Prewar apartments

Many Bronx apartment buildings went up in the 1920s and 1930s. Their plaster walls, arched openings and decorative details look wonderful when they are prepared well, and show every flaw when they are not. We patch cracks, sand rough spots and prime before painting, and we hand cut lines around arches and moldings.

## Co-ops

In co-op buildings we follow the board's rules for work hours and elevator use, protect the halls, and keep your neighbors happy.

## Houses

In houses we can paint a single room, one floor or the whole home. Near the water, we recommend finishes that resist moisture in bathrooms, kitchens and basements.

You get one flat-rate price for the whole job, agreed in writing before we start.
