Long Island homes are generally bigger than city apartments, and the work is planned differently. A typical job here is several rooms or a whole house, often with a tall entry, an open living area and a long stairway.

## Nassau County

From Garden City and Rockville Centre to Great Neck, Syosset and Massapequa, Nassau has Colonials, Tudors, Capes, ranches and split-levels. Older homes often have plaster walls and original trim. Newer homes usually still have their builder-grade flat paint, which scuffs easily. A repaint in a washable finish makes busy rooms easier to keep clean.

## Western Suffolk

In Huntington and nearby, we paint historic homes, village apartments and small shops. Older houses there often need extra prep on trim and plaster, which we include in the quote.

## Near the water

In Long Beach and the South Shore, damp air is hard on paint, especially in bathrooms, kitchens and basements. We recommend moisture-resistant finishes for those rooms.

Every job gets one written, flat-rate price covering prep, paint, labor and cleanup, with no hourly surprises.
