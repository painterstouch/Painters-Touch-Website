Brooklyn homes tend to have character, and that character takes care to paint well. Brownstones and limestones have tall ceilings, deep moldings and stair halls that run three or four floors. Prewar apartments have plaster walls and original woodwork. Lofts and new condos have huge, flat walls where any unevenness shows.

## Brownstones and townhouses

Older townhouses are where prep matters most. Woodwork often has years of paint built up, and plaster walls have settled and cracked. We fill, sand and prime so the finish is smooth, then cut sharp lines along crown molding and ceiling medallions.

## Prewar apartments

In walk-ups and elevator buildings, we protect shared halls and stairs, carry everything up and down carefully, and keep to the hours the building allows.

## Lofts and new buildings

In open spaces, consistency is everything. We plan the order of walls so each one is finished in a single pass, which avoids the patchy look that large walls can get.

You get one flat-rate price for the whole job, covering prep, paint, labor and cleanup.
