Queens has some of the most varied housing in the city, and each type paints a little differently. In a single week we might repaint a prewar one-bedroom in Astoria, a stairwell in a two-family house in Sunnyside and a glass-walled condo in Long Island City.

## Older apartments and co-ops

Buildings from the 1920s through the 1950s usually have plaster walls with decades of paint on them. Hairline cracks, rough patches and old nail holes show through a new coat if they are not fixed first. We patch, sand and prime before painting, which is where most of the difference in the final finish comes from.

## Houses and two-family homes

In houses, stairwells and tall hallways are often the hardest part of the job. We plan for ladders, protect the stairs and floors, and paint the whole house or just the floor you need, such as an upstairs unit between tenants.

## New condos

Newer buildings have smoother walls but more rules. We follow the building's work hours, reserve the elevator, and keep halls and lobbies clean and protected.

Whatever the building, you get one written, flat-rate price for the whole job before we start.
