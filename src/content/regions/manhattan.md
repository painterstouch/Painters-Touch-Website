Painting an apartment in Manhattan is as much about planning as it is about paint. Co-op and condo buildings usually have rules for contractors: approved work hours, service elevator bookings, protection for halls and lobbies, and sometimes paperwork for the managing agent. We sort these out with you before we schedule the job, so there are no surprises on the first morning.

## Prewar apartments

Prewar layouts often have plaster walls, high ceilings, picture rails and detailed moldings. These look beautiful when they are prepared properly. We patch and skim cracks, sand old trim smooth and prime repairs before the finish coats go on.

## Postwar and new condos

Newer buildings usually have drywall, which is quicker to prepare, but they often have stricter rules about noise, hours and deliveries. We work quietly and keep the job contained.

## Townhouses

In townhouses, tall stair halls and many floors take extra planning. We protect stairs and floors throughout and can paint the whole house or a few rooms at a time.

Every job gets one written, flat-rate price that covers prep, paint, labor and cleanup.
