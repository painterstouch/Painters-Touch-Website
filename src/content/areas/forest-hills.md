Forest Hills is known for its mix of leafy streets, Tudor-style houses and large prewar co-op buildings. The painting work here tends to be about detail: staircases with spindles, wood window casings, crown molding and doors that have been repainted many times.

For houses, we plan the job around the architecture. Stairwells and tall entry halls get the right ladders and floor protection, and trim is sanded, filled and caulked before painting so edges look sharp. In co-op apartments, the building usually sets rules for contractors, so we ask about work hours, elevator access and any paperwork when you request a quote.

Whether it is one bedroom or the whole apartment, you get one written flat-rate price before we start.
