Astoria is a neighborhood of mixed housing: prewar apartment buildings near the train lines, brick rowhouses on the side streets, and two-family homes where owners often live upstairs and rent below. Each one calls for a slightly different approach to painting.

In the apartment buildings, walls are often plaster, and years of repainting leave rough edges and hairline cracks. We sand, patch and prime before we paint, so the new color sits on a smooth surface. In the houses, stairwells and tall hallways usually make up a big part of the job, and we plan for the ladders and floor protection they need.

If you are repainting before renting out a unit, tell us your move-out and move-in dates. We will schedule the work to fit and give you a single flat-rate price for the whole job, with no hourly surprises.
