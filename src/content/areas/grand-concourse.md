The Grand Concourse is one of the Bronx's most recognizable streets, lined with apartment buildings from the prewar era, many with Art Deco touches. Inside, the apartments tend to have solid plaster walls, high ceilings and trim that has been painted many times.

That calls for patient prep. We scrape and sand rough trim, patch cracks in the plaster, and prime the repairs before the first coat of paint goes on. Skipping those steps is why many repaint jobs in older buildings look fine for a few months and then start to flake or crack.

We work with owners repainting their own apartments and with landlords and managers who need units ready for the next tenant. Either way you get a flat-rate price per unit, a clear start date, and a clean finish.
