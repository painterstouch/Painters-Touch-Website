Williamsburg's housing and workspaces are some of the most varied in the city: converted factories with soaring ceilings, new condo buildings, old walk-ups and storefronts that double as studios. The painting challenges follow the buildings.

In lofts, the walls are big and open, and a patchy finish shows across the whole room. We paint full wall sections in one pass so the color and sheen stay even from corner to corner. Where there is exposed brick, we help you decide whether it is better left as it is. In studios, shops and cafes, we schedule around when you are open.

As always, you get a single flat-rate price for the full scope, with prep and clean-up included.
