Throgs Neck is a residential corner of the Bronx where one- and two-family houses sit close to the water. Most painting jobs here are home projects: a few bedrooms before a baby arrives, a whole floor before a tenant moves in, or a refresh of the entire house.

Being near the water matters indoors, too. Bathrooms, basements and rooms with poor airflow collect moisture, and paint applied over a damp or mildewy wall will not last. We check surfaces before painting, clean and prime where needed, and use paints suited to damp rooms.

For two-family homes, we can coordinate the upstairs and downstairs units so shared stairs and entries are painted once and finished properly. Everything is priced as one flat-rate quote.
