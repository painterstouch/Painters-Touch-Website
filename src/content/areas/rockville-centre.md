Rockville Centre is a village with a walkable downtown and tree-lined streets of traditional houses. Colonials, Tudors and Capes are common, and inside you often find hardwood floors, wide baseboards, crown molding and paneled doors.

That is the kind of detail that makes a painting job look great or look rushed. We spend our time on it: covering the floors properly, sanding and caulking the trim, and cutting clean lines where walls meet molding.

Near the village center, we also paint apartments, both for owners and for landlords turning units between tenants. Every job is quoted as a flat-rate price before we start.
