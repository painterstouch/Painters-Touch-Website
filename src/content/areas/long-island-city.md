Long Island City has changed fast. Along the waterfront and around Court Square, tall residential buildings sit next to older rowhouses, converted industrial lofts and small commercial spaces. Painting in each of these is a different kind of job.

In the high-rises, the paint is only half of it. Elevator bookings, building work hours and rules about protecting hallways all shape the schedule, so we ask about them before we quote. The walls in newer buildings are usually smooth and in good shape, which means a clean repaint, often in a more durable finish than the original, goes quickly.

In lofts and commercial spaces, the walls are bigger and the priority is keeping the space working. We paint in stages, finish each section completely, and clean up before we leave.
