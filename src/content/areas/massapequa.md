Massapequa is known for its postwar family houses: ranches, Capes and split-levels on tidy residential streets. Many of them have been updated over the years, and many have not, which makes for a wide range of painting jobs.

Some customers want a refresh: new colors in the main living areas, clean white trim, and a fresh start in the bedrooms. Others are working through the leftovers of an older house, like dark wood paneling or glossy finishes that need a proper prep before they will take new paint.

After a renovation, new drywall takes primer and a careful first coat. We plan that into the job so the finished walls look even and the sheen is consistent.
