Todt Hill is known for its large houses on wooded, sloping lots. A painting job here is usually a big one: many rooms, formal spaces and long staircases.

The best way to handle a large house is a clear plan. We walk the rooms with you, agree on the scope, and give you a single flat-rate price. Then we sequence the work so one area is finished and protected before the next begins, which keeps the disruption to the household small.

Formal rooms with molding, paneling or wainscoting get extra attention. Sanding, filling and priming come first, then a smooth finish that shows off the woodwork instead of hiding it.
