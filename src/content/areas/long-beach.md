Long Beach sits on a barrier island, with the ocean on one side and the bay on the other. The mix of condos, apartment buildings and houses reflects that, and so do the painting challenges: salt air, humidity and homes that stand empty for part of the year.

Moisture is the main thing. Paint does not stick well to damp or dirty walls, so surfaces are cleaned and checked before we paint. In bathrooms and kitchens we recommend washable, mildew-resistant finishes, which last longer and are easier to keep clean.

For condos and second homes, we can plan around your calendar: before the summer season, between rentals, or while you are away.
