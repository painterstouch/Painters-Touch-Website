Syosset is a family-oriented community of larger homes, many with open floor plans and tall entries. These houses are comfortable to live in and demanding to paint: high walls, big rooms, and finishes that need to stand up to everyday use.

Tall foyers and great rooms are where equipment matters. We bring what the height requires and protect the floors and stairs first. Higher walls also show roller marks more, which is why we keep a wet edge and finish each wall in one session.

If your house still has its original builder-grade paint, a repaint is a good chance to move to a washable finish in the rooms that get the most use.
