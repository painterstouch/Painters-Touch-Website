Many Upper East Side homes are in buildings with front-desk staff, service entrances and clear expectations for contractors. That makes the way we work as important as the paint itself: arriving when we say we will, protecting what we pass through, and leaving the common areas as we found them.

Inside the apartment, detail is what people notice. Well-sanded, evenly finished trim and doors, sharp lines along crown molding and a wall finish with no roller marks are what make a space feel considered. We spend our time on that rather than rushing through it.

For townhouses, the work spans floors and stairwells, and the order of work matters. We sequence it so each floor is finished and protected before the next begins.
