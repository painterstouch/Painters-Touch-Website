In Garden City, interior painting jobs tend to be larger and more spread out than in the city: whole floors of bedrooms, a living room and dining room, a two-story entry and the staircase that connects them. The right approach is to treat the house as one project.

We walk through the rooms, agree the scope and give you a flat-rate price. Then we work room by room, so you can keep using the rest of the house, and we protect floors and furniture in the room we are in. Wood trim, paneled doors and built-ins get sanded, filled and caulked before they are painted, so the finish lasts.

Because there is no building schedule to follow, we can fit the job around your family's calendar, including a move-in or a family event on the horizon.
