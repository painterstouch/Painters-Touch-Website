St. George sits at the northern tip of Staten Island, where the ferry terminal brings commuters in and out every day. Around it, you find some of the borough's older housing: historic houses with porches and wood details, row houses, and apartment buildings of different ages.

Older houses reward good preparation. Original trim and doors usually have many coats of paint on them, and the walls can have settled cracks. We scrape, sand and repair first, and prime before painting, so the finish looks sharp and lasts.

For rental units near the ferry, we can paint between tenants on a schedule that fits your turnover, with a flat-rate price for each unit.
