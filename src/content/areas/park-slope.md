Walk through Park Slope and you see the reason it is so popular for painting work: rows of brownstones and limestones with ceilings that can run well over ten feet, ornate cornices, wide staircases and plaster walls with a century of paint on them.

That history is what makes the prep so important. Old trim often has many layers of paint that need scraping and sanding before a new coat will look right. Plaster walls develop hairline cracks that should be repaired, not painted over. And a tall parlor-floor ceiling takes proper equipment and a patient hand.

We price all of that into a flat-rate quote after seeing the rooms, so the job is not billed by the hour, however many ladder trips it takes.
