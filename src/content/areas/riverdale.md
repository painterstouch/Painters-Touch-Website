Riverdale feels different from the rest of the Bronx: hilly streets, tall trees and a mix of large co-op buildings and stone or Tudor-style houses. That variety shapes how we paint here.

In the apartment buildings, the rooms are often larger than in a typical city apartment, with long hallways and plenty of trim. The work is the same careful routine, only more of it, and the building usually has contractor rules about hours, elevators and protecting hallways. We ask about all of that when you request a quote.

In the houses, the details carry the job. Wood trim, staircases and built-in shelves need sanding and filling before paint, and the steep lots often mean stairs between levels. We plan the equipment and the order of rooms for the house in front of us and give you one price for it.
