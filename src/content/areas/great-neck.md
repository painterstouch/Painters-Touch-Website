Great Neck combines two very different kinds of painting work. Around the village center there are apartment buildings, co-ops and condos with their own rules. On the quieter streets and along the shore there are large single-family homes with formal rooms and generous entries.

For apartments, we focus on logistics as much as paint: building hours, elevator reservations and keeping shared hallways clean. We ask for those details up front and schedule accordingly.

For houses, the work is about scale and finish. More rooms, taller walls and trim that will be seen up close. We plan the order of work so the house stays livable and give you one flat-rate price for the whole job.
