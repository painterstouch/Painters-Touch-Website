Huntington is where our service area reaches Suffolk County. It is a historic harbor town with a lively village center, and the painting work reflects that: older houses with plenty of character, apartments above shops, and small businesses that want a fresh look without closing for long.

In older homes, preparation is the main event. Original trim, plaster walls and years of paint all need to be repaired and sanded before the finish goes on. We take that time, and we put the cost in a flat-rate quote so there are no surprises.

In the village center, we paint around your hours. Retail and office spaces are done in sections, and each area is cleaned up before the next one begins.
