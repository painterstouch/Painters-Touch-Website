Great Kills is a family-oriented part of Staten Island, with streets of detached houses and attached townhouses not far from the harbor and the park. Most of our work in neighborhoods like this falls into a few patterns: refreshing a house after moving in, painting before a sale, or updating a few rooms that have gotten tired.

For a move or a sale, timing is what matters. If you have a closing or a listing date, tell us when you ask for a quote. We build the schedule backward from it and keep you informed on progress.

Wherever the stairs are, we cover them. Houses with multiple levels take more time to protect and to paint around, so we include it in the price from the start.
