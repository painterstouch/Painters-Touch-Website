## What lime wash looks like

Lime wash gives walls a soft, matte, slightly cloudy look, with subtle changes in tone from one patch to the next. Instead of a perfectly flat color, you get something that looks layered and lived-in. It is popular in living rooms, bedrooms and entry halls, where the walls are a big part of how the space feels.

## Where it works best

Plaster walls, archways, rounded corners and ceilings are where hand-applied finishes really show. The same warm color can run across the walls, the ceiling and the curve of an arch, which makes a space feel continuous and cozy. It also works well as a single accent wall if you want to start small.

## How we apply it

Preparation comes first. We repair what needs repairing, sand it smooth and prime with a primer that suits both your walls and the finish. Floors, furniture and trim are covered and taped off before any color goes on. Then the finish is built up by hand in thin layers, so the depth and movement come from the way it is applied. Because it is hand work, we plan extra time for it and keep the layers even from one wall to the next.

## Choosing your color

Color looks different on a wall than it does on a chip, especially with a finish that changes with the light. We help you narrow down a color, and we recommend a test patch on your own wall so you can see it in your light before the whole room is done.

## What to expect

A hand-applied finish has character: small variations are part of the look, not a flaw. It is also more delicate than standard washable paint, so touch-ups are handled differently. We will walk you through how to care for it when the job is done. If you want a different specialty finish, tell us what you have in mind and we will let you know honestly whether it is a good fit.
