## Fast, clean repaints between tenants

When a tenant moves out, every empty day costs you rent. Tenant turnover and move-in painting is all about getting the unit fresh, clean and ready to show without dragging it out. Give us your move-out and move-in dates, and we'll schedule the job to fit.

An empty apartment is the fastest kind of apartment to paint. No furniture to move, no one to work around, just walls, ceilings, trim and doors. (Honestly, it's the closest thing painters have to a vacation.)

## Built for landlords and property managers

If you manage one unit or a whole building, you want three things: speed, consistency and no surprises. That's how we run turnovers:

- **We schedule around your dates.** Move-out, repairs, paint, then move-in.
- **One flat price per unit,** in writing, before we start. No hourly billing.
- **The same colors, every time.** Most landlords we work with want white, sometimes a brighter white, sometimes a creamier one, but white. We call it "landlord white," and we've painted a lot of it.
- **Prep that holds up.** Nail holes, dents and scuffs from the last tenant get patched and sanded, so the next tenant doesn't move in to someone else's picture-frame layout.

Two-family homeowners in Queens and Brooklyn often rent out one floor. We can paint just that unit, the shared stairwell, or both.

## Move-in painting

Just bought a place or signed a lease? Paint before the boxes arrive. Move-in painting is faster, cleaner and usually less expensive than painting around furniture later, and you get to start life in your new home with fresh walls instead of the last owner's color choices. (No judgment. Okay, a little judgment.)

## Getting a listing photo-ready

Realtors and sellers: fresh, neutral walls photograph better and show better. We patch the wear and tear buyers notice, paint in colors that work for most buyers, and keep to your listing date.

## Working in NYC buildings

Co-ops and condos have rules for contractors: approved work hours, service elevator bookings and protection for hallways and lobbies. We plan the job around them, so your building manager stays happy and your neighbors barely know we were there.

## How to get a turnover quote

It starts with a phone call or text, so we can understand the unit and your timeline. Send us photos of each room and the unit size, plus your dates. From there, we either price it from the photos or schedule a quick visit. Either way, you get one written, flat-rate price.
