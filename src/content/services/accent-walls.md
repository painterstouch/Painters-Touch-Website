## Why an accent wall works

A single wall in a bold color gives a room a focal point. It adds depth, frames the furniture in front of it, and makes the lighter walls around it look cleaner. It is also one of the simplest ways to change a space without repainting everything.

## Choosing the wall

The best accent wall is usually the one you see first: the wall behind the sofa, the wall behind the bed, or a tall wall that draws the eye upward. In rooms with high ceilings or beams, a deep color can show off the architecture instead of hiding it.

## Choosing the color

Color looks different in your light than it does on a chip. We help you narrow it down, look at it in the room, and consider the floors, the furniture and the trim that will sit next to it. Deep teals, greens and blues look dramatic against white trim and ceilings, while warm tones feel softer.

## Where the quality shows

With an accent wall, the edges are everything. The line along the ceiling, the corner where the color meets a white wall, and the trim at the floor all need to be sharp. We mask and cut in carefully, and we protect your floors and furniture before we start.

## Prep matters more with deep colors

Dark colors show every flaw in the wall underneath. We patch and sand first, then prime with a primer that suits the color, so the finish looks even and rich rather than patchy. All of that is included in your flat-rate quote.
