## Why prep matters

Fresh paint makes small flaws more obvious, not less. A dent, a crack or a patch that was not sanded flush will catch the light and show through even the best paint. Good drywall work is invisible once the wall is painted, which is the whole point.

## What we repair

We fill nail holes and anchor holes, repair dents and dings, re-tape cracked or lifting seams, and patch larger holes so the repair blends into the surrounding wall. Each repair is built up in thin layers and sanded smooth between coats.

## Sealing and priming

Patched areas absorb paint differently from the rest of the wall, which can leave dull spots called flashing. We prime repairs before painting so the finish looks even. Stains are sealed so they do not bleed through.

## When we recommend a specialist

If a wall is wet, moldy or structurally damaged, painting over it is the wrong answer. We will say so, and we will tell you what needs to be fixed before we come back to finish the job.

## Included with painting jobs

Basic patching and sanding is part of the prep for every painting job we quote. This service covers jobs where the repair work itself is the main task.
