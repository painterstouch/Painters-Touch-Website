## Walls

Walls make up most of what you see in a room, so this is where a clean finish matters. We move furniture to the center of the room and cover it, lay protective coverings on the floors, and mask edges before any paint is opened. Dents and nail holes are patched and sanded smooth, repairs are spot-primed, and the paint goes on in even coats with sharp, straight lines where walls meet ceilings and trim.

## Ceilings

We paint ceilings first so nothing drips onto freshly painted walls. A flat finish is the usual choice because it hides imperfections and cuts glare. In kitchens and bathrooms, where steam and splashes are a factor, a more washable finish can be a better fit. We will recommend one when we see the room.

## Trim and doors

Trim is slower work than walls, and it shows. We sand, fill and caulk baseboards, crown molding, window casings, door frames and doors, then finish them in a durable satin or semi-gloss that stands up to scuffs and wipes clean. Replacing a tired, yellowed trim color with a crisp one is often the quickest way to make a whole room look new.

## Prep is most of the job

A paint job lasts as long as the preparation under it. That means covering what should not be painted, repairing what should not be seen, and priming where the surface needs it. We build all of this into your flat-rate quote, so you are never billed extra by the hour for doing the job properly.

## How pricing works

You send us the rooms and a few photos. We give you one written, flat-rate price for the whole job. If you decide to add or change the scope, we agree on the new price before the work happens.
