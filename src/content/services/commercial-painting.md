## Less disruption, same careful finish

A shop that is closed or an office that cannot work costs money, so scheduling is part of the job. We ask about your hours, your landlord's rules and any deadlines, then plan the work in sections so the space stays usable wherever possible.

## Finishes that hold up

Commercial spaces take more wear than a bedroom does. High-traffic walls, door frames and trim need finishes that can be cleaned and touched up. We recommend finishes based on how the space is used: a reception area, a stockroom and a conference room do not need the same paint.

## What we paint

We paint walls, ceilings, trim, doors and frames in offices, retail shops, showrooms and small suites. Patching, sanding and priming are part of every quote, so surfaces are ready for paint rather than painted over.

## Protecting your space

Fixtures, flooring, counters and equipment are covered or moved before we start, and the space is cleaned at the end of each working period so you can open or get back to work.

## A clear price up front

Commercial customers usually need a number they can approve. We give you one flat-rate written price for the entire scope, covering prep, paint and clean-up.
