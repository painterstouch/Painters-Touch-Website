Ask a painter what interior painting costs and you'll hear "it depends" (we know, we roll our eyes at that answer too). So here are real numbers.

**Interior painting cost typically runs $2 to $6 per square foot of floor space, or about $2,000 to $6,000 for every 1,000 square feet, based on [NerdWallet's 2026 figures](https://www.nerdwallet.com/home-ownership/home-improvement/learn/save-on-cost-to-paint-a-house).** Most single rooms cost $400 to $1,500. In New York City, expect the higher end of those ranges.

Below we break down the cost per square foot, per room and for a whole house. We also cover what pushes the price up, why NYC costs more, and how to read a quote without getting burned.

## Interior painting cost per square foot

The $2 to $6 per square foot range is based on your home's **floor area**, not the wall surface. It's the number most painters and cost guides use for a quick estimate, and the top-ranking cost guides all land on it.

Where you fall in that range depends on what's included:

- **Around $2 per sq ft:** walls only, in good condition, same or similar color.
- **Around $4 per sq ft:** walls, ceilings and trim, with normal patching.
- **$6 and up per sq ft:** dark color changes, high ceilings, lots of trim, damaged walls or specialty finishes.

A word of warning: **cheap square-foot pricing can be misleading.** A room with smooth, freshly painted walls isn't the same job as a room with peeling paint, cracked plaster and a deep red you want gone. A per-foot price that ignores the condition of your walls is a guess, not a quote.

On the **labor cost to paint a 12x12 room**: CertaPro's Boston-area guide puts a standard 12x12 bedroom at $700 to $900, including walls, ceiling, baseboards, doors and windows. Most of that is labor. A bedroom only needs a few gallons of paint.

Bathrooms are small but fiddly, with lots of cutting in around fixtures, plus moisture-resistant paint. Kitchens cost more once cabinets are involved, and cabinet painting is a separate job entirely.

## What drives the cost of interior painting?

Every competitor guide lists square footage first. Fair enough. But in our experience, these are what actually move the number:

1. **Wall condition and prep.** Holes, cracks, stains and peeling paint all need fixing before paint goes on. This is where cheap quotes cut corners, and it's where the real money is earned. When Elena F. hired us to paint her whole apartment, every ceiling had popcorn texture. [Skim coating](/services/drywall-repair-prep/) them added work, and it's the part she raved about.
2. **Color changes.** Going from landlord white to deep green is a bigger job than white to white. Dark colors over light (or light over dark) often need a tinted primer.
3. **Number of coats.** Two coats is our rule on every job, because that's what it takes for full color and even sheen. (If you're asking whether it needs a second coat, it needs a second coat.)
4. **Ceiling height and access.** Ten-foot prewar ceilings, stairwells and two-story foyers need taller ladders or scaffolding, and more time.
5. **Trim, doors and built-ins.** Old trim can take longer than the walls. Decades of built-up paint and failed caulk don't fix themselves.
6. **Specialty finishes.** [Lime wash](/services/lime-wash-specialty-paint/) and decorative finishes cost more than basic painting, because they take more skill.
7. **Water stains and cracks.** A stain needs its moisture source fixed and a stain-blocking primer, or it bleeds right back through. A crack that keeps coming back may need more than filler.

## Why interior painting costs more in NYC

Brick Underground put the average NYC rate at **$5.25 per square foot back in 2022**, near the top of the national range, and prices have risen since. The paint costs the same here. What adds up is everything around it:

- **Building rules.** Co-op and condo buildings set work hours, require service elevator bookings, and often require painters to carry large insurance policies. All of that is time and overhead.
- **Walk-ups.** Every ladder, drop cloth and gallon goes up the stairs by hand.
- **Prewar walls.** Plaster with decades of paint layers needs more patching and priming than new drywall.
- **Tall ceilings and detailed trim**, common in [Manhattan](/areas/manhattan/) and [Brooklyn](/areas/brooklyn/) prewar apartments.

That's also why national per-room averages can look low to New Yorkers. They usually assume a house with a driveway, not a fifth-floor walk-up.

## How much of the cost is paint?

Less than you'd think. Labor is most of what you pay a painter for. But the paint still matters, because **cheap paint is a false economy.** Bargain paint covers poorly, so you need more of it, plus more coats and more labor, to get the same result.

That doesn't mean you need the most expensive can on the shelf. A quality midrange line is often the right call. We use Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore and choose the product for the room, not the price tag.

## DIY vs hiring a painter

Painting it yourself saves the labor, which is the biggest part of the bill. You'll still pay for paint, primer, rollers, brushes, tape and drop cloths, and you'll spend a weekend or two on it.

**Worth doing yourself:** a single room, an [accent wall](/blog/accent-wall-painting/), or a refresh in the same color.
**Worth hiring out:** ceilings, tall walls and stairwells, plaster repairs, popcorn ceilings, dark color changes, whole apartments, and anything on a deadline like a move-in or listing date.

## How to compare painting quotes

The cheapest painting quote can become the most expensive mistake. A painter who skips sanding, primer or proper repairs saves you money up front, and then you see it in every wall. When you compare quotes, check that each one spells out:

- **Prep and repairs:** what gets patched, sanded, caulked and primed.
- **Paint:** the brand, the product line and the number of coats.
- **Scope:** walls only, or ceilings, trim and doors too.
- **Protection and cleanup:** furniture, floors and daily cleanup.
- **Price type:** flat rate or hourly. Flat rate means the number doesn't change if the job runs long.

A single number with no scope makes quotes impossible to compare. For what it's worth, we give every customer one written, [flat-rate price](/services/interior-painting/) covering prep, paint, labor and cleanup, and we never change it without your approval.

## The bottom line

Interior painting cost comes down to size, wall condition and what's included. Budget $2 to $6 per square foot nationally, expect the higher end in NYC, and compare quotes on scope, not just price.
