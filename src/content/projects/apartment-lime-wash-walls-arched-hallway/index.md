Hand-applied lime wash in soft peach and blush tones. In the hallway, the same warm color runs across the walls, the archway and the ceiling, which gives the narrow space a cozy, continuous feel. The finish has the cloudy, layered depth that flat paint can't match, and the crisp white doors and trim stay sharp against it.
