A light gray room in a Forest Hills home, with dents and dings all over the walls, became a calm, earthy sage green. Every hole and dent was patched and sanded first, with the floors covered and taped. Then the walls went sage green, and the white crown molding, chair rail and baseboards now really stand out against it.
