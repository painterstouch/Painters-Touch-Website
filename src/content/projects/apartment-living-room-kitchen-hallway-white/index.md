A clean, bright white finish carried through the living space, the kitchen and the hallway of this apartment. The smooth, even coverage and crisp edges along the ceilings, soffits, doors and baseboards make every room feel lighter and more open, and they let the warm hardwood floors and the wood-look cabinets stand out.
