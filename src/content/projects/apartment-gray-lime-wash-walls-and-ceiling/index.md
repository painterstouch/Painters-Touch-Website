A soft gray lime wash carried across the walls, ceilings and hallway of a high-rise apartment overlooking the Hudson River. Wrapping the whole room in one finish gives it a calm, cloud-like depth, and every brushstroke catches the light differently through the day, while the white baseboards keep the edges crisp.
